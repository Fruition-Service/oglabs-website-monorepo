PROCESS OPTIMISATION

Find the friction, then design it out

Most teams lose hours every week to processes that grew by accident. OG Labs analyses how work actually happens in your business, identifies the bottlenecks and manual steps, and redesigns your workflows for efficiency, then implements the technology and automation to make the improvement stick.

What we deliver

Process Mapping & Audit

Document your current workflows end-to-end, identify bottlenecks, duplicate efforts, and manual steps that should be automated or eliminated.

Automation Opportunity Analysis

Prioritise automation opportunities by ROI and feasibility, then build a structured roadmap to implement improvements systematically.

Implementation & Measurement

Execute process improvements with clear KPIs — typically achieving 40–60% reduction in manual tasks and measurable cost savings within 6 months.

Our approach

1. Map

We document your current processes and find where time, effort and value leak out through observation and stakeholder interviews.

2. Analyse

We identify bottlenecks, duplicate effort, manual handoffs and automation opportunities with a clear view of the cost of each.

3. Redesign

We design leaner workflows around your goals, testing the design against edge cases before implementation.

4. Implement

We put the new process into your tools, with automation where it earns its place.

5. Measure

We track improvement against agreed metrics and refine until targets are met.

What improvements look like

Fewer handoffs

Every manual step between people is a place where work can stall. We reduce handoffs to only the ones that genuinely need human involvement.

Less rekeying

Data entered once flows automatically to every system that needs it, eliminating a major source of errors and wasted time.

Clear ownership

Every step has a defined owner and a clear trigger, so nothing sits in limbo waiting for someone to realise it is their job.

Process optimisation FAQs

Do we need new software to optimise our processes?

Not always. Sometimes the biggest gains come from redesigning the process in tools you already have. We only recommend new tools when they earn their place against a clear ROI.

How do you measure success?

Against metrics agreed at the start: time saved per process, cycle time, error rate and cost per transaction. We baseline before we start so the improvement is measurable.

How quickly will we see results?

Quick wins often land within the first two to three weeks. Larger process redesigns are phased so value arrives early rather than waiting for everything to be perfect.
